## Purpose

Extend the human-accepted Stage 1A/1B experimental dependency with only the elementary geometry

fixes the inner-product argument order, and proves its pointwise gradient formula.

`AcceptedStage1B` is supplied as a read-only module and already imports `AcceptedStage1A`. Do not
redeclare, modify, or strengthen any accepted definition or theorem.

## Ambient setting and conventions

Continue inside namespace `Issue2Autoformalization.Stage1A`.

For the definition and its two elementary evaluation rules, let $E$ be a real inner-product space
with:

- `NormedAddCommGroup E`; and
- `InnerProductSpace ℝ E`.

For the two gradient declarations, additionally assume `CompleteSpace E` so that the real Hilbert
Riesz identification used by Mathlib is available.

No measurable space, measure, probability normalization, finite moment, finite-dimensionality, or
nontriviality assumption belongs to Stage 2A. In particular, every declaration must remain valid
when $E$ is the zero-dimensional space.

The linear test in direction $v$ is

$$
f_v(x)=\langle v,x\rangle_{\mathbb R}.
$$

The argument order is intentional: use `inner ℝ v x`, not `inner ℝ x v`. The codomain and scalar
field are both exactly `ℝ`.
