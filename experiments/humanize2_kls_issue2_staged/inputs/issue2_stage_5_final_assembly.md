## Frozen boundary semantics

### Deliberate hypothesis redundancy for statement fidelity

`linearTestLower` and `linearTestLowerCertificate` deliberately carry
`[FiniteDimensional ℝ E]` and `[IsProbabilityMeasure μ]` even though the accepted underlying
theorems do not require them. The final packaged statement quantifies over finite-dimensional
probability measures, so these theorems are frozen to match that statement rather than to be
hypothesis-minimal. This is an intentional exception to the usual minimality discipline. Keep
both instances exactly as displayed; they are required hypotheses, not smuggled assumptions.

### Euclidean and zero-dimensional boundary

`euclideanLinearTestLower` instantiates `E := EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin D)` with free `D : ℕ`,
including `D = 0`. Every Hilbert, measurable, Borel, and finite-dimensional instance for this
space must come from Mathlib's canonical instances; add none by hand. The zero-dimensional case
remains included end to end, with the accepted value `lambdaMaxCovariance μ = 0`.
