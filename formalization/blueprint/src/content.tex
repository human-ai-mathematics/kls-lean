\begin{abstract}
This document is the dependency map for the canonical Poincar\'e foundation in
\texttt{kls-lean}.  It separates what Lean already certifies, what is stated but not yet
formalized, and what still needs a statement decision. Public interfaces may evolve at every
stage; proof work does not require a prior freeze. Four sufficient KLS routes are recorded
as alternatives rather than conjunctive prerequisites.  They share analytic infrastructure; no
logical or probabilistic independence is asserted.
\end{abstract}
