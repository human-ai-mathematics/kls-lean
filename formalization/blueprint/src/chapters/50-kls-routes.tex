\chapter{Four routes to KLS}
\label{chap:kls-routes}

This chapter is a roadmap imported from the pinned upstream commit \sourcecommit.  Its nodes have
different upstream statuses: open gates, conditional implications, and proof dossiers marked
\texttt{proved}/\texttt{checked\_by: agent}.  Those statuses are reported route by route.  None is
Lean-certified, so no node in this chapter carries \texttt{leanok}.

The TeX command \texttt{\textbackslash uses} means logical AND.  The four sufficient routes,
however, are alternatives.  Their logical composition is therefore recorded in
\texttt{route-composition.yaml}: the target is an \texttt{any\_of}, while the named nodes inside
each route form an \texttt{all\_of}.  In particular, \cref{conj:kls} intentionally has no
\texttt{uses} edge to all four routes.  This logical OR asserts neither mathematical independence
nor probabilistic independence; the routes share the bridges of
\cref{chap:shared-analytic-bridges}.

\section{Eldan localization}
\label{route:eldan-localization}

The route follows the evolution of a balanced fixed cut under stochastic localization, with a
two-color covariance/excess decomposition.  The live gate is weaker than the older all-cut
Carleson package.  Eldan introduced the underlying scheme \cite{Eldan2013ThinShell}; Lee and
Vempala obtained quantitative isoperimetric, concentration, and mixing bounds toward KLS
\cite{LeeVempala2024}.  Neither paper proves the tight-prefix gate below or the full KLS
conjecture.

\begin{assumption}[Tight-prefix absorptive Carleson estimate]
  \label{ass:tight-prefix-carleson}
  There are universal $\eta\in(0,1/6]$, $T_0,C_0,C_1>0$, and $\alpha<1$ such that, for every
  isotropic log-concave $\mu$, every fixed cut $E$ with
  $\abs{\mu(E)-1/2}\le\eta/2$, and every $0<T\le T_0$,
  \[
    \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}S_t\dd t
    \le C_0T+C_1\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}r_t\dd t
       +\alpha\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}D_t\dd t.
  \]
  Here $S_t,r_t,D_t$ and the tight stopping time $\tau_\eta$ are the two-color quantities of the
  pinned localization manuscript.
\end{assumption}

\begin{corollary}[Tight-window consumption]
  \label{cor:tight-window-consumption}
  \uses{ass:tight-prefix-carleson,thm:logconcave-cheeger-poincare-comparison}
  The tight-prefix estimate controls the stopped scalar Riccati evolution and yields the
  dimension-free boundary lower bound for every balanced near-minimizing cut.  The standard
  balanced near-minimizer reduction then yields KLS when the assumption holds universally.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
  Integrate the stopped Riccati identity, absorb the $D_t$ term using $\alpha<1$, and apply
  Gronwall.  The resulting uniform stopped bound feeds the localization survival estimate.  This
  proof is upstream agent-reviewed, not Lean-certified.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Historical refuted weighted package}
\label{route:eldan-weighted-package}

The following package is retained only to make a failed historical branch auditable.  It is not a
fifth route alternative and is not an input to the live tight-prefix package.

\begin{assumption}[Refuted literal weighted near-Cheeger package]
  \label{ass:weighted-package}
  There are universal constants $T_0,C_0,C_1,C_2,\gamma>0$,
  $\eta\in(0,1/4]$, and $\beta\in[0,1/2)$ with $2\beta+64\eta^2<1$ such that every balanced
  finite-perimeter cut with initial excess $e_0\le1$ satisfies both a superlinear weighted-excess
  estimate
  \[
    \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}
      e_t\bigl(1+\norm{A_t}_\op\bigr)^{5/2}\dd t
      \le C_2\bigl(Te_0+T^{1+\gamma}\bigr)
  \]
  and the paired absorptive weighted Stein-trace estimate, uniformly for $0<T\le T_0$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Exponential-spectator obstruction]
  \label{prop:weighted-spectator-obstruction}
  For every proposed $C,T_0,\gamma>0$, $\eta\in(0,1/2)$, and $\delta>0$, some balanced cylinder
  in an isotropic product of centered one-sided exponentials has additive and relative initial
  excess at most $\delta$ but violates the displayed weighted-excess estimate at a time
  $0<T\le T_0$.  Thus \cref{ass:weighted-package} is false with its stated global quantifiers.
  The product witnesses do not refute KLS.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
  The pinned upstream proof uses an independent exponential spectator to make the global
  covariance weight large while preserving the cut geometry.  It is agent-reviewed, not
  Lean-certified.
\end{proof}

\section{Moment-map spectral route}
\label{route:moment-map-spectral}

This route tracks a normalized first eigenfunction through localization, retaining the directional
posterior covariance information that a scalar covariance bound discards.

\begin{question}[Fixed-eigenfunction full-damping occupation]
  \label{q:mm-spectral-occupation}
  On smooth strongly log-concave isotropic approximants, do universal $T_0,C_0,C_1>0$ exist such
  that the normalized first eigenfunction quantities $g_t,H_t,A_t$ satisfy, for $0<t\le T_0$,
  \[
    \E\int_0^t\norm{H_s}_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\dd s
    \le C_0t+C_1\E\int_0^t\abs{g_s}^2\dd s
       +\E\int_0^t2g_s^\mathsf{T}A_sg_s\dd s,
  \]
  uniformly through approximation?
\end{question}

\begin{proposition}[Full-damping sufficiency]
  \label{prop:spectral-sufficiency}
  \uses{q:mm-spectral-occupation,thm:selected-literature-constant-equivalence,
        thm:affine-isotropic-reduction}
  If the occupation estimate holds, set
  \[
    M_*=(1+C_0T_0)e^{C_1T_0},
    \qquad
    T_* = \min\{T_0,(2M_*)^{-1}\}.
  \]
  Then every isotropic log-concave law satisfies $\CPLit\le2/T_*$.  Hence the estimate implies
  KLS; no strict damping surplus or convergence of eigenfunctions is needed.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
  It\^o's formula for $\E\abs{g_t}^2$ contains the Hessian occupation and the full damping term
  with opposite signs.  The hypothesis cancels the latter, Gronwall bounds the remaining moment,
  and posterior variance conservation gives the spectral-gap estimate.  This is an upstream
  conditional argument only.
\end{proof}

\section{Moment-map CMH route}
\label{route:moment-map-cmh}

The CMH route is a sufficient-condition program, not a reformulation of KLS.  It asks only for one
well-chosen regular recovery sequence with bounded canonical moment-Hessian quotient.  The regular
moment-map background is classical work of Berman--Berndtsson and Fathi
\cite{BermanBerndtsson2013RealMA,Fathi2019SteinMomentMaps}; the recovery-envelope gate below is a
separate repository statement.

\begin{assumption}[Bounded CMH recovery envelope]
  \label{ass:cmh-recovery-envelope}
  There is a universal $C$ such that every centered log-concave probability $\mu$ admits a
  regular recovery sequence $(\mu_k)$ converging to $\mu$ in ambient $W_2$ and
  satisfying
  \[
    \liminf_{k\to\infty}\CMH(\mu_k)\le C.
  \]
\end{assumption}

\begin{corollary}[One CMH recovery sequence suffices]
  \label{cor:cmh-recovery-sequence-suffices}
  \uses{ass:cmh-recovery-envelope,thm:selected-literature-constant-equivalence,
        thm:affine-isotropic-reduction}
  Under the bounded recovery-envelope assumption, every centered log-concave law satisfies the
  universal affine Poincar\'e inequality.  Consequently the assumption implies KLS, including
  degeneration to a proper affine support.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
  Apply the regular-class CMH-to-affine-Poincar\'e endpoint to the recovery sequence and pass to
  the limit using the upstream lower-semicontinuity theorem.  This bridge is upstream
  agent-reviewed, not Lean-certified.
\end{proof}

\section{Conditional-fiber frame route}
\label{route:conditional-fiber-frame}

The frame may depend on the measure, but it must be chosen before the test function.  The route is
existential over all admissible frames; failure of the natural simplex root frame does not refute
it.

\begin{definition}[Conditional-fiber form]
  \label{def:conditional-fiber-form}
  For an isotropic law $\mu$ on $\R^d$, let $\rho$ be an even probability law on the sphere with
  \[
    d\int_{S^{d-1}}\theta\theta^{\mathsf T}\dd\rho(\theta)=I_d.
  \]
  Disintegrate $\mu$ along lines $z+\R\theta$ and define
  \[
    \mathcal D_{\mu,\rho}(f)
    =d\int_{S^{d-1}}\int_{\theta^\perp}
      \frac{\Var_{\mu_{\theta,z}}(f(z+T\theta))}
           {\Var_{\mu_{\theta,z}}(T)}
      \dd\bar\mu_\theta(z)\dd\rho(\theta),
  \]
  with null or zero-variance fibers contributing zero.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Conditional-fiber form and factor-four bridge]
  \label{lem:conditional-fiber-form}
  \uses{def:conditional-fiber-form,thm:one-dimensional-logconcave-poincare,
        thm:selected-literature-constant-equivalence}
  For a full-dimensional log-concave probability with density and finite second moment, the form
  is densely defined, closed, symmetric, Markovian, and reversible.  Moreover, for locally
  Lipschitz $f$ in its form domain,
  \[
    \mathcal D_{\mu,\rho}(f)\le4\int\norm{\nabla f}^2\dd\mu.
  \]
  Thus a form gap $\Var_\mu(f)\le C\mathcal D_{\mu,\rho}(f)$ implies
  $\CPLit(\mu)\le4C$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  The one-dimensional conditional Poincar\'e estimate bounds each fiber variance; isotropy of the
  frame averages directional derivatives to the full squared gradient.  The closed-form and
  reversibility assertions use conditional expectation.  The proof dossier is marked
  \texttt{proved}/\texttt{checked\_by: agent} upstream; it is not Lean-certified here.
\end{proof}

\begin{question}[Test-independent conditional-fiber frame]
  \label{q:conditional-fiber-frame}
  \uses{def:conditional-fiber-form}
  For every full-dimensional isotropic log-concave $\mu$ on $\R^d$, is there an admissible frame
  $\rho_\mu$, fixed independently of $f$, and a universal $C$ such that
  \[
    \Var_\mu(f)\le C\mathcal D_{\mu,\rho_\mu}(f)
  \]
  for every $f$ in the maximal closed form domain?
\end{question}

An affirmative answer together with \cref{lem:conditional-fiber-form} implies KLS.  There is no
upstream theorem labelled \texttt{thm:conditional-fiber-frame-suffices}; the route sidecar uses
the actual two-node conjunction instead of inventing one.
