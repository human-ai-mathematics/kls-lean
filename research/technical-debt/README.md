The historical register describes earlier trees and narrower verification scopes. Its counts
and former paths are not current inventory. The later layout work does not reopen its resolved
findings or advance mathematical proof or certificate status. Use [HANDOFF](../../HANDOFF.md)
for snapshot-specific acceptance pointers.

For a new finding, record source evidence, mathematical responsibility, consumers, proposed
placement and validation under the [classification workflow](../workflow.md). Distinguish a
confirmed problem, a candidate, an accepted exception and a resolved issue. File length and
cross-family imports alone do not justify a split or relocation. Keep existing IDs and dated
evidence intact; a proposed change is accepted only after its clients and required checks pass.
