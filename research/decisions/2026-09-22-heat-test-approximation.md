## Proved partial results

For a closed densely defined real Hilbert-space operator `T`, write `A = T†T` and `P_t`
for its generated semigroup. No tilted-law, smooth-potential or convexity assumption is
needed for these three results in `Generated/Smoothing.lean`:

1. `pow_three_mul_norm_map_adjointComp_apply_sq_le`: for every `g` and `t > 0`,
   `t³ ‖T A P_t g‖² ≤ 4 ‖g‖²`. Domain witnesses are explicit parameters; the existing
   positive-time domain theorems provide both for arbitrary `g`. They are not extra
   regularity assumptions on the concrete heat datum.
2. `tendsto_map_apply_of_pos`: if `gₙ → g` in the initial Hilbert space, then
   `T P_t gₙ → T P_t g` for each fixed `t > 0`.
3. `tendsto_map_adjointComp_apply_of_pos`: under the same hypotheses,
   `T A P_t gₙ → T A P_t g`.

The bound follows by splitting time in half: `A P_t g = P_{t/2} A P_{t/2} g`,
`t ‖T P_{t/2} h‖² ≤ ‖h‖²`, and `(t/2) ‖A P_{t/2} g‖ ≤ ‖g‖`. Applying the bounds
to differences proves continuity. These are Hilbert-space norm estimates, not pointwise
or essential-supremum gradient estimates; no Bakry–Émery conclusion is used.

In the full-dimensional log-concave model, with arbitrary weight `B`, the fourth theorem
`HeatSemigroup.exists_seq_smooth_tendsto_regularized_fields` gives, for every `g ∈ L²`
and `r > 0`, smooth compactly supported `fₙ` such that, for `Fₙ = P_{r/2}[fₙ]`,

```
T Fₙ → T P_r g,       T A Fₙ → T A P_r g.
```

Its construction uses the existing form core to approximate `P_{r/2} g` by `[fₙ]`,
then the two new continuity theorems and the semigroup law. It requires no potential
assumptions at all. It is deliberately named `regularized_fields`, not a higher-order core.

## Exact remaining obligation and route failures

**The requested full heat-test extension is not proved.** For the current unit-weight,
smooth convex tilted model, a sufficient remaining statement for the original route is:
for every `g ∈ L²` and `r > 0`, there exist `qₙ ∈ C_c^∞` such that

```
[∇qₙ] → T P_r g,       [-∇(ℒ qₙ)] → T A P_r g       in L²(ν; V).
```

Only these two field convergences are required by the existing transfer. A stronger,
sufficient density theorem would make `C_c^∞` dense in
`{F ∈ D(A) : AF ∈ D(T)}` for `‖F‖ + ‖AF‖ + ‖T(AF)‖`. That density theorem is not
proved here, and the `C¹` deficiency-element regularity theorem is not such a theorem.
Smoothness of `U` makes `ℒ(C_c^∞) ⊆ C_c^∞`; it supplies no graph-norm density argument
at this higher order. No bounded higher derivatives of `U` have been assumed.

The constructive attempt above solves the field-convergence half after positive-time
regularization, but fails precisely at applying smooth-data Bochner to `Fₙ`: the library
does not produce `Fₙ = [qₙ]` with `qₙ ∈ C_c^∞`. Simply removing `P_{r/2}` destroys the
second-field control. Proving the desired inequality for heat evolutions of smooth
compact initial data would be another sufficient remaining step, but is also unproved.

The alternative routes were inspected, not completed or implemented: positive bounded
test approximants still need control of both their values and generator images suitable
for pairing with `L¹`; the generator-form route still needs the nonsmooth product rule
and a justified limit of `∫ (Afₙ) Γ(Tfₙ,Tψ)`. Bounds on `ψ` and `Aψ` alone do not supply
a bound on `Tψ`. The new Hilbert-space estimate supplies no such bound either. No
semigroup gradient estimate is used to close that gap.
