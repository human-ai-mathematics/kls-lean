## Correction to the Previous Evaluation

In the previous round I wrote the gap as "there is no way to transition from closed forms to `P_t = exp(-tL)`". This statement overreached for both the theorem's validity and feasibility, and has been revised in `tasks.json`. The fact is: the generation theorem indeed does not exist (no `C0Semigroup`, no Hille–Yosida, no functional calculus for unbounded self-adjoint operators), **but all the components required by the classical Yosida construction are present**.

| component | location |
| --- | --- |
| Lax–Milgram (resolvents of bounded coercive forms) | `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LaxMilgram.lean` |
| Riesz representation | `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Dual.lean` |
| bounded element `exp` | `Analysis/Normed/Algebra/Exponential.lean` |
| Adjoint and Closability of Unbounded Dense Operators | `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap.lean` |

Key point: The energy form is unbounded on `L²`, but Lax–Milgram is used **on the form domain**—on the `D(T)` graph norm, where `B_s(u,v) = ⟪u,v⟫ + s⟪Tu,Tv⟫` is both bounded and coercive. Thus, unboundedness does not constitute an obstacle.

The form side of this repository is already in place: the energy is presented as a locally compact closable operator `T : H₀ →ₗ.[ℝ] H₁` (`Energy/Relaxation/LinearPMap.lean`, `WeightedGradient.lean`, `ClosedForm.lean`), `E(f) = ‖T f‖²` being a closed form.

## Goal

Given a densely defined closed operator between real Hilbert spaces `T`, construct the self-adjoint contraction strongly continuous semigroup generated by `L = T*T` `P_t = exp(-tT*T)`
And its generator laws. **All is general Hilbert space content**, without measures, without log-concave, without KLS——
This is intentional: the gap is the only part of the Buser program that can independently contribute to Mathlib upstream.
