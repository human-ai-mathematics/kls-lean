# Analysis and BKL filenames (2026-10-12)

Proposed record path: `research/decisions/2026-10-12-analysis-bkl-filename-layout.md`.

The proposed reorganization is limited to the supplied Analysis root and KLS/BKL root. Three related operator modules move to `Analysis/Operator/` as `Coordinates.lean`, `Positive.lean`, and `InverseSqrt.lean`. This family is intentionally separate from `Analysis/Matrix/`: operator coordinates bridge finite operators and matrices, positive-operator results retain generic and finite-dimensional scope, and the principal inverse square root is an operator construction. `RingInverseDerivative.lean` remains a generic root-level normed-algebra tool.

The six BKL suspension modules move to `KLS/BKL/Suspension/`, with `Basic.lean` as the genuine foundational entry and `Law.lean`, `Density.lean`, `Moments.lean`, `Cumulant.lean`, and `Derivatives.lean` as responsibility-specific extensions. No aggregate or compatibility module is introduced. The suspension family remains separate from the BKL cumulant envelope and from the analytic second- and conditional-third-cumulant clients.

`LocalThirdCumulant.lean` is retained: local positive exponential radius is a meaningful distinction from all-radius variants; conditional status belongs in the statement and progress record. `PosteriorDomain.lean` is renamed `PosteriorCovariance.lean` because covariance is the module's primary mathematical object; the coherent posterior boundary is not split. `GaussianTaylor.lean`, `Proposition71.lean`, `SecondCumulant.lean`, `PosteriorResponse.lean`, `CumulantEnvelope.lean`, and `Reduction.lean` remain at the BKL root for their concrete or source-locator roles.

These are pure file moves or filename renames. Namespaces, declaration names, theorem statements, definitions, proof bodies, assumptions, options, import order, public visibility, mathematical scope, and certificate status remain unchanged. Current imports, consumers, metadata, evidence paths, Blueprint links, guards, snapshots, hashes, and live documentation must be updated. Historical decisions, handoffs, explorations, and prior migration maps must not be rewritten. Compiler-private and hygienic owner-prefix changes require an explicit path/declaration-owner map and structural type and definition-closure comparisons.

Complete map: [file and module map](../api-migration/2026-10-12-analysis-bkl-filename-map.json). `sigma` is defined in Law; Basic defines the potential using a sigma parameter and the foundational convexity properties. The independent subset acceptance excludes the proposed ConditionalThirdCumulant rename. Whole-file source preservation and structural type/definition closure comparisons precede evidence refresh. Full verification is required; this decision does not claim it already passed.
